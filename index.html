Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Enter with your Bedrock gamertag and one contact method. There is no CAPTCHA or email verification.

Can I enter more than once?

One entry per gamertag and browser device ID for each giveaway. Changing browsers or clearing storage can change the device ID; it does not prove a person’s identity. Duplicate or false entries may be disqualified.

Who can see my contact details?

Only authorized admins. Public results show winning gamertags, the prize and draw details. We don’t verify ownership of the contact account, so double-check your details.
